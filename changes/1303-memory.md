**`@Memory` replaces `@Compaction`, `uncompactedTurns`, `memoryStrategy` and `@agboss/core/memory`** (#1303). What a workflow, an agent or a model router remembers of its thread is one `@Memory` class (`-memory`), named by `memory` on `@Workflow`, `@Agent` and `@Router` (the lowest level wins): `ttl` (required: a duration or `Ttl.Forever`), `uncompactedTurns` (default 5), `turnsBetweenCompactions`, and what is older — `compaction: { strategy, prompt?, modelProvider?, model?, summaryQueueLength?, summaryMaxTokens? }`, or a `compact(input)` method on the class (sync or async, no `implements`; `compaction` and `compact()` together do not compile and fail assembly with `[memory.conflicting-compaction]`). `NoMemory` (built in) on an agent or a router sees no earlier turn.

Removed: `@Compaction`, `CompactionStrategy.None` and `.Custom`, `CompactionHandler`, `CompactionOptions` and its variants, `CompactionError` (it is `StepError.Memory`: `memory`, `details.summaryMaxTokens`, the failure as `cause`), `@Application({ compaction, uncompactedTurns })`, `@Workflow({ compaction, uncompactedTurns })`, `@Agent({ compaction, uncompactedTurns, memoryStrategy })`, `@Router({ compaction, uncompactedTurns })`, `@agboss/core/memory` (`BaseMemoryStrategy`, `SlidingWindowStrategy`, `HistoryTurn`, `MemoryView`, …), the rules `[compaction.*]` and `[memory.not-a-strategy]`, `ComponentKind.Compaction`. Added: `Memory`, `NoMemory`, `MemoryOptions`, `DeclarativeMemoryOptions`, `CodeMemoryOptions`, `MemoryCompaction`, `SummaryQueueCompaction`, `SingleSummaryCompaction`, `ComponentKind.Memory`, `onCompactionStart` / `onCompactionEnd` (`CompactionStartEvent`, `CompactionEndEvent`) on `@Observer`, the rules `[memory.not-a-memory]`, `[memory.invalid-setting]`, `[memory.uncompacted-turns-invalid]`, `[memory.conflicting-compaction]`.

Renamed: `TurnLimit.ALL` → `TurnLimit.All`, `TokenLimit.MODEL_MAX` → `TokenLimit.ModelMax`, `CompactionTurn.{startName, finishName}` → `{start, finish}`, `Compacted.compaction` → `Compacted.memory` (`summaryQueueLength` is optional: only a queue reports it), `Summary.compaction` → `Summary.memory`, `Summary.bundle` / `Turn.bundle` → `workflow`, the `compaction` argument of `latestSummaries`, `summaryCount` and `uncovered` → `memory`, the spend caller `compaction:<name>` → `memory:<name>`, the agent snapshot's `compaction` → `memory`. A spend after a finished turn by an agent's own strategy is gone with the strategy.

Store follow-up: `Turn.bundle` / `Summary.bundle` → `workflow` and `Summary.compaction` → `memory` in the PostgreSQL and MongoDB stores' mappers.
