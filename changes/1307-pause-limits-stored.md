section: Breaking
issue: 1307
---

**A pause carries the limits it was opened under** (#1307, #1310): `PauseRecord` has `firstAskedAt`, `maxExpiresAt?` (the target's `escalationLimits.maxTotalTimeout` counted from the first asking, by the target's calendar) and `escalationLimits` (`maxExtensions?`, `maxReassignments?`; `{}` when the target names none), written when the pause is opened. Every check reads the record, never the application's present configuration: `app.setPauseTimeout`, `app.reassignPause`, an expiry's `PauseExpiration.reassign`, `ExpiredContext.maxExpiresAt` and the store's atomic counts. A deploy that renames or removes a target, or loosens or drops its `escalationLimits`, changes nothing for a pause already waiting (and a target that left the chain's history no longer drops out of its limits). A move to another target narrows them — the smaller of the pause's own and the destination's, per field, the destination's total wait counted from the first asking by its calendar for the new assignees — in the same write that makes the replacement; a change in place narrows nothing. Stores (`@agboss/core/stores`): `PauseTimeoutChange.maxExtensions`, `PauseInPlaceReassignment.maxReassignments` / `maxExtensions` and `PauseReplacement.maxReassignments` / `maxExtensions` are removed — a store reads `pause.escalationLimits` itself — and `PauseReplacement.to.limits` (`PauseLimitsOffer`: `maxExpiresAt?`, `escalationLimits?`) is the destination's offer, which `reassignByTenantIdAndPauseId` narrows with `narrowedLimits` (new, in `/stores`) in the replacement's write and judges the move against; the contract has cases for the stored fields, the narrowing and the atomic counts. `StoredPause` requires `firstAskedAt` and `escalationLimits`: a pause record without them is `StoreError.UnreadableRecord`, never read as "no limit". A store that keeps the record as JSON needs no migration for new pauses; a store that maps record fields to its own columns or documents must keep the three fields. No reader of an earlier shape: **before upgrading, answer, cancel or let expire every pending pause** — a pause record written by an earlier version is unreadable, and the scans that read it (the expiry, reminder and re-send looks) stop at the first such row, so no pause behind it in the scan order expires or is reminded until it is gone. The store also refuses, in the write, a deadline past the record's `maxExpiresAt` (or none while a cap is set), and a stored `maxExpiresAt` / `firstAskedAt` that is not the text `toISOString()` writes is unreadable.
