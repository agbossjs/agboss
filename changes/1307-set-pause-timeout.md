---
section: Breaking
issue: 1307
---

**`app.extendPause` is `app.setPauseTimeout`; `Timeout.None`; `permitTimeoutChange` is enforced** (#1307). `await app.setPauseTimeout({ pauseId, timeout, reason? }, { actor })` moves an open pause's deadline to `now + timeout` — a `WorkingDuration`, `Milliseconds` or `Timeout.None` (new, `@agboss/core` beside `Ttl`: it takes the deadline away, and its reminder) — later **or earlier**, and returns `PauseTimeoutChangeResult { expiresAt?, previousExpiresAt? }` (`Date`s, absent when there is none). `app.extendPause`, `ExtendPauseRequest` and `PauseExtension` are removed (no alias). A `timeout` below `seconds(1)` is `PauseError.InvalidTimeout` (no longer a `DtoInvalid`); a pause that is closed or past its deadline is `PauseError.Closed`; a deadline past the run's or a quorum's, the chain's `maxTotalTimeout` or `maxExtensions` (and `Timeout.None` under any of them) is `PauseError.EscalationLimitExceeded`; a pause whose first `send` has not returned is `PauseError.UpdateContended` (the channel never hears `onPauseTimeoutChanged` before `onPauseOpened`). The target's `permitTimeoutChange(ctx)` — `actor`, `timeout`, `currentExpiresAt`, `reason`, `input`, `trail` — is called before anything is written and only `true` permits: `false`, a throw, a rejection, any other value or an unresolvable target is `PauseError.PermissionDenied`; `[pause.permit-hook-not-enforced]` is removed. The `reason` (PII-masked) and the actor are a `timeout-changed` entry of the pause's chain of custody; `remindAt` is made again from the new deadline, none when it is not after now. The stored notice kind `extended` is `timeout-changed` (no reader of the old value), and `ChannelPauseTimeoutChangedEvent`, `ChannelListenerTimeoutChangedEvent` and the observers' `PauseTimeoutChangedEvent` name `expiresAt` / `previousExpiresAt` only when there is one. Stores (`@agboss/core/stores`): `extendByTenantIdAndPauseId` is `changeTimeoutByTenantIdAndPauseId`, `PauseDeadlineChange` is `PauseTimeoutChange` (`expiresAt?`, `previousExpiresAt?`, `reason?`) — the "only later" rule is dropped, the compare-and-set over `previousExpiresAt` (absent: none) and over `seen` (required: trail length, assignees, deadline, `isSent` — `isAsSeen` / `seenOf` are in `/stores`; a deadline that moves both ways is no version of the pause), "after the moment of the write" and the `maxExtensions` count stay; the contract has cases for shortening, taking away, a lost compare-and-set and `maxExtensions`.
