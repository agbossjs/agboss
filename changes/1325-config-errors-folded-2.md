**Twelve more configuration errors are variants of `ConfigError`** (#1325b): `GraphRuleError` is `ConfigError.GraphRules` (`violations`; `config.graph-rules`, was `graph.rules`), `ConfigurationError` is `ConfigError.ModelSettings` (`problems`), `EnvironmentError` is `ConfigError.Environment` (`config.environment`; the reason moved from `error.code` to `error.envCode`, an `EnvironmentErrorCode`, and the option is `envCode`, not `code`), `EvalCaseInvalidError` is `ConfigError.EvalCase` (`suite`, `issues`; `config.eval-case`, was `eval.case-invalid`), `NotAWorkflowStartError` is `ConfigError.NotAWorkflowStart` (`config.not-a-workflow-start`, was `workflow.not-a-start`), `WorkflowLoadError` and `ApplicationLoadError` are `ConfigError.WorkflowLoad` and `ConfigError.ApplicationLoad` (`config.workflow-load`, `config.application-load`, were `load.workflow`, `load.application`), `RagScopeError` is `ConfigError.Component`, `ModelKindError` is `ConfigError.ModelKind` (`model`, `modelKind`, `call`; `config.model-kind`, was `model.wrong-kind`), `ToolCallingUnsupportedError` is `ConfigError.ModelKind` and `ProviderCapabilityError` and `ModelListError` are `ConfigError.ModelProvider`. The classes and their `…Options` types are removed from `@agboss/core`; the sentences are as they were. `DecisionError` now lives in its own file of the families. `agboss` keeps its own CLI codes (`graph.rules`, `application.load`, …) in the error envelope, and an environment problem still reports `environment.not-found`, `environment.missing-variables`, … as its code.
