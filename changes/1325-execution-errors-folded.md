section: Breaking
issue: 1325

**Nine execution errors are variants of `ExecutionError`** (#1325b): `UnknownThreadError`, `InvalidThreadIdError`, `ThreadIdsExhaustedError`, `ThreadOwnerError`, `DuplicateExecutionIdError`, `InvalidMintedIdError`, `OutputInvalidError`, `IncompatibleResumeError` and `CompensationFailedError` are removed, with their `…Options` types, from `@agboss/core`. They are `ExecutionError.UnknownThread` (`execution.unknown-thread`, was `run.unknown-thread`), `ExecutionError.InvalidThreadId` (`rule`; `execution.invalid-thread-id`), `ExecutionError.ThreadIdsExhausted` (`generator`, `tries`; `execution.thread-ids-exhausted`), `ExecutionError.ThreadOwnerMismatch` (`execution.thread-owner-mismatch`, was `run.thread-owner`), `ExecutionError.DuplicateExecutionId` (`generator`; `execution.duplicate-execution-id`), `ExecutionError.InvalidMintedId` (`generator`, `method`, `rule`; `execution.invalid-minted-id`), `ExecutionError.OutputInvalid` (`execution.output-invalid`, was `step.output-invalid`), `ExecutionError.IncompatibleResume` (`threadId`, `executionId`, `paused`, `current`; `execution.incompatible-resume`, was `resume.incompatible`) and `ExecutionError.CompensationFailed` (`failures`; `execution.compensation-failed`, was `compensation.failed`). `.onError(ExecutionError.CompensationFailed, …)`, `error.kind` and `error.code` take the place of `instanceof` and the old codes; the sentences are as they were. The `details` of a thread-ids or duplicate-id error no longer repeat the `method`.
