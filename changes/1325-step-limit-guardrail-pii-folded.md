---
section: Breaking
issue: 1325
---

**The step, limit, guardrail and PII errors are variants of `StepError`, `LimitError`, `GuardrailError` and `PiiError`** (#1325b): `PaidStepError`, `StepFailedError`, `LimitExceededError` and every class below them are removed from `@agboss/core` with their `…Options` types — `AgentFailedError`, `AgentAnswerInvalidError`, `QualityGateError`, `GuardFailedError`, `ActionFailedError`, `QuestionFailedError`, `RouterFailedError`, `RouterDecisionError`, `ArbiterError`, `QuorumNotReachedError`, `ChildExecutionFailedError`, `PauseExpiredError`, `ToolBlockedError`, `RerankFailedError`, `ModelFileLimitError`, `FileRedactionFailedError`, `GuardrailBlockedError`, `PiiRefusedError`, `PiiDetectionFailedError`, `BudgetExceededError`, `DeadlineExceededError`, `StepsLimitExceededError`, `VisitsLimitExceededError`, `DepthLimitExceededError`, `HopsLimitExceededError` and `MeshLoopDetectedError`. They are `StepError.Agent`, `.AgentAnswerInvalid`, `.QualityGate`, `.Guardrail` (`guardrail`, `reason`; `step.guardrail`, was `step.guard`), `.Action`, `.ExternalTask` (`externalTask`; `step.external-task`, was `step.question`), `.Router`, `.RouterDecision`, `.Arbiter`, `.QuorumNotReached`, `.Child`, `.PauseExpired` (`pauseKind`), `.ToolBlocked`, `.RerankFailed`, `.ModelFileLimit`, `.FileMaskingFailed`; `GuardrailError.Blocked` (`guardrail`, was `guard`); `PiiError.Refused` (`piiKind`, was `kind`) and `PiiError.DetectionFailed`; `LimitError.Budget`, `.Deadline`, `.Steps` (`workflow` is the name, `workflowRecord` the record), `.Visits`, `.Depth`, `.Hops` and `.MeshLoop` (`entry` is the line `application › start`, `meshEntry` the object, `callChain`), and `LimitError.Agent` for an agent's own limits (`maxModelCalls`, `maxToolCalls`, …). `.onError(StepError, …)` and `skip()` take exactly what `StepFailedError` took (no limit, guardrail, PII, cancel or hand-over), `.onError(LimitError, …)` any limit; a variant is no longer a subclass of another (`StepError.RouterDecision` is not a `StepError.Router`, `StepError.QualityGate` not a `StepError.Agent`). The `step.failed` link of an error record's lineage is gone: it is `[variant code, "step", "agboss"]`. A step error made inside its step names it itself (`component`, `nodeId`, `visitNumber`, `itemIndex`), and `usage` stays out of `details`.
