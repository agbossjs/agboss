**Eval: judges are classes, one spend split, report schema 5** (#1328): `@EvalSuite({ judges, tools, modelProvider? })` and `evalCase({ judges, tools, input, pauses, expectedStatus })` take `@Judge` classes; `checkTrajectory`, `judgedBy`, `EvalCheck`, `JudgeEvalCheck`, `TrajectoryEvalCheck` and `EvalSuiteOptions.checks` are removed (a case's `tools` always run first). A single-turn case is `input` (with its `pauses`); `answerPause(Target, decision, { actor? })` and `replyToPause(Target, reply, { actor? })` resolve a pause of a turn; the last turn must end as `expectedStatus` (default `Finished`), else the run fails with `execution.status-mismatch`. `EvalSuiteOptions.modelProvider` pins the model judges' provider (judge, then suite, then application; a candidate workflow's own binding of a judge is no longer used). `ExpectedToolCall.arguments` and `EvalExpectedToolCallTrace.arguments` are `input`, `ToolArgumentsOf` is `ToolInputPartialOf`. `EvalRunResult.spentUsd` and `EvalRunSpend` are `{ executionUsd, judgeUsd, totalUsd }` (a baseline's `avgSpendUsd` is the execution spend); `EvalCheckResult.kind` (`EvalCheckKind`); `EvalExecutionTrace.status`; `EvalPauseTrace` is `{ nodeId, target, resolution, actor?, output? }`; `startedAt`/`endedAt` of reports, comparisons and steps are `Date`s (ISO 8601 in the file); `EvalRunReport.judges`. `EVAL_RUN_SCHEMA_VERSION` is 5 and the compact baseline's schema is 2; older files are refused, not converted.
