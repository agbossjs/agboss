section: Breaking
issue: 1328
---

**One unit runner, `testUnit`, and `services` in place of `override`** (#1328, part b): `testUnit(Class, options?)` runs any component on its own — a tool, an action, a router, an arbiter, a judge, a knowledge base, a guardrail, a memory's compaction, a tool interceptor, a role's reply interpreter; `testUnit([PolicyA, PolicyB])` is the PII policies one element lists. It replaces `testTool`, `testAction`, `testRouter`, `testArbiter`, `testRag`, `testJudge`, `testPii`, `testGuardrail`, `testToolInterceptor`, `testCompaction` and `testReplyInterpreter`. `testDto(Dto)` gives `{ validate, mask, jsonSchema }` of a DTO; `testService(Class)` runs a multi-method service (`.service`, `.run`, `.get`, `.mockService`, `.start()`, `.stop()`). `override(Class, Replacement)` and the `overrides` option of `testWith` and the unit runners are gone: `services: [Replacement]` takes a fake that `extends` the real class or names its port with `injectAs`, and `provide(TOKEN, value)`; `AutoApproveChannel.for(Role)` and `AutoRejectChannel.for(Role)` answer a channel role. `testWith(App, { only: [Workflow] })` assembles just those workflows and their children. `TestApp.get(token)` gives a class, a token's value or a `defineEnv` dictionary's values (`app.get(appEnv)`). `streamWith(chunks)` and `failStreamAfter(chunks, failure)` script a model's stream. Added: `testUnit`, `testDto`, `testService`, `UnitRunnerOf`, `DtoTestHarness`, `ServiceRunner`, `streamWith`, `failStreamAfter`, `StreamTurn`, `StreamFailureTurn`, `TestWithOptions.only`, `TestWithOptions.services`, `UnitOptions.services`, `TestApp.get`. Removed: `testTool`, `testAction`, `testRouter`, `testArbiter`, `testRag`, `testJudge`, `testPii`, `testGuardrail`, `testToolInterceptor`, `testCompaction`, `testReplyInterpreter`, `override`, `Override`, `overrides`.
