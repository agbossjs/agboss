---
section: Breaking
issue: 1343
---

**`@Observer` replaces `@Injectable` + `implements On…` on execution observers, and the 33 single-hook `On*` interfaces are removed** (#1343): an observer is `@Observer({ name?, description?, workflows?, agents?, deps? })` on a class with a method per hook; `@Application({ observers })` is the only place observers are registered, and a listed class without `@Observer` is `[observer.not-an-observer]` (a repeated name is `[observer.duplicate-name]`, a misspelled `on…` method is still `[observer.unknown-hook]`). `workflows` and `agents` pass only the events of those workflows or agents to the observer, so a hook needs no `if (event.workflow.name !== nameOf(W)) return;`; app-level events reach only observers without that filter. `ExecutionObserver` stays, with every hook optional. Removed: `OnAgentStart`, `OnAgentEnd`, `OnActionStart`, `OnActionEnd`, `OnRouterStart`, `OnRouterEnd`, `OnToolStart`, `OnToolEnd`, `OnGuardrailStart`, `OnGuardrailEnd`, `OnJudgeStart`, `OnJudgeEnd`, `OnModelStart`, `OnModelEnd`, `OnRagStart`, `OnRagEnd`, `OnChannelStart`, `OnChannelEnd`, `OnExecutionStart`, `OnExecutionEnd`, `OnExecutionError`, `OnExecutionPause`, `OnExecutionResume`, `OnExecutionRejected`, `OnExecutionLeaseTakeover`, `OnCompensationStart`, `OnCompensationEnd`, `OnFencedEffect`, `OnOutboundCall`, `OnQuorumDecided`, `OnStepHandedOver`, `OnValuesOutdated`, `OnLaunchItemsTruncated`; `AuditLogHooks`, `OBSERVER_HOOKS`, `ObserverClass`, `ObserverHook` and `HookResult` move to `@agboss/core/internal`.

**New `onAgentRetry(event: AgentRetryEvent)`** (`agent`, `reason: AgentRetryReason` — `InvalidAnswer`, `InvalidToolInput`, `JudgeRejected` —, `attempt`, `maxAttempts`, `feedback`): one call each time an agent's own retry loop corrects itself.

**Event fields renamed**: `ToolStartEvent` has `tool`, `agent?` (absent for a standalone tool call) and `input` (was `toolName`, `agentName`, `arguments`); `ToolEndEvent` has `tool`, `agent?` and `output` (was `update`); `JudgeStartEvent` / `JudgeEndEvent` have `judge` and `agent` (was `name`, `agentName`) and `JudgeEndEvent.output` (was `update`); every `EndEvent` (`AgentEndEvent`, `RouterEndEvent`, `RagEndEvent`, `GuardrailEndEvent`, `ActionEndEvent`, `ChannelEndEvent`) has `output` instead of `update`; `ExecutionEndEvent.output` is new (what the finish produced). Model events keep their names until #1313.
