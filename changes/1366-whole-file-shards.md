**Every file that holds a number, a limit, a deadline, a step count, spend, a ledger id, a fence or a cancel is mutated whole, and what it was not mutated for is tested** (#1366, acceptance S and T of the budget and ledger groups): the shards of the 48 files named by acceptance R and of every file that was mutated in part now take each of them from its first line to its last — the "no spend is decided there" ranges that hid a launch branch's step (`branchVisitOf`: a branch counted as no step, so `maxSteps` let a paid call through) and an inner quorum's deadline (`withQuorumDeadline`: not capped by the outer quorum's) are gone. Both are killed by tests of their own: a run with `maxSteps: 9` that refuses its tenth paid call, and an inner quorum whose deadline is the outer one's when that is earlier. `PARTLY_SHARDED_SRC` is gone with the ranges; a declaration left out of a mutated file is named in `wholeFile(file, { name: reason })` (`scripts/stryker-ranges-whole.mjs`) with why it holds none of those, and `tests/docs/mutation-shard-coverage.test.ts` reads that list. The tests that the whole files asked for cover the side path a failure takes, a paused run's loops, the SQLite connection's busy retry, the retention option, the implementations' choice, a step's frame and its idempotency key, the mesh's seam and replay window, the stall bound of a stream, the A2A client's reads, the policy set's masks and the framework's error classes. The stale `#stopDeadline` entry of `cancel-x-runs` is out of `scripts/stryker-proven-hangs.json`. No behaviour changes: four rewrites that remove a branch no input can tell apart (`withQuorumDeadline`'s two-instant minimum, a router's request with no hidden route, an `AIMessage`'s empty `invalid_tool_calls`, an error's `details`) are listed in the pull request.
