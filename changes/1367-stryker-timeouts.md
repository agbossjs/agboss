**A Stryker Timeout in a mutation shard is a hang, not a hidden survivor** (#1367, agbossjs/mongodb#103): Stryker's limit for one mutant is `timeoutFactor` x the net time of the tests that cover it + `timeoutMS` (1.5 x, 20 s), and the net time leaves out hooks and setup, so a mutant no test kills could not finish within it, read as a Timeout, and counted as killed. `scripts/stryker-timeouts-ms.mjs` now gives each shard its own `timeoutMS` (3 x the shard's overhead on CI, 60 s at least; a shard without one fails to load) beside a `timeoutFactor` of 3, so the limit is 3 x the shard's whole run, the dry run being one worker and the mutants three. `scripts/stryker-timeouts.mjs` fails a shard's job in `ci.yml` and `mutation-nightly.yml` on any Timeout the time limit ended, and on any RuntimeError or CompileError, that is not in `scripts/stryker-proven-hangs.json` (shard, file, mutator, replacement, an anchor of the line, kind, reason; never an id or a line), and on an entry that matches no mutant of its shard; a Timeout of Stryker's hit limit (a loop that does not end) is counted, not listed, because whether it ends that way or in a failing test is a race. The CI cache of each shard's incremental run is keyed by the limits and the vitest configs, so a change of them runs every shard cold. The `pauses`, `pauses-sqlite` and `rate-limits` shards no longer set `testNamePattern`, which vitest keeps as an override of the pattern Stryker sets for each mutant, so every mutant ran every test of the shard: the conformance files leave the other contracts out by the shard (`MUTATION_SHARD`). Every `vitest.mutation*.config.ts` sets `testTimeout` and `hookTimeout`. The shards re-run cold under it found one survivor, the clock of the root's unwind (`deps.clock ?? systemClock`), killed by a test. Test-only and CI changes; no product behaviour changes; no migration.
