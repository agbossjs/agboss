**Every store call is bounded** (#928): a call the framework awaits that the store never answers no longer waits for ever. Under a held lease it is bounded by the time the holder still trusts the lease and, unanswered, hands the tree over with its outcome unknown: the lease is abandoned (never released, it expires in its store) and the next holder reconciles — the fenced effect's row is replayed, a pending outbound intent is reconciled with the other system, the latest checkpoint is resumed; nothing is charged twice. Any other call (the sweep's look, a child's claim, `acquire`, `requestCancel`, an answer to a pause, A2A) fails with `StoreCallTimeoutError` after 15 s; retention deletes and A2A file bodies after 5 minutes. A lease renewal that does not answer is not a hand-over: renewals are bounded at 5 s and now run every quarter of the lease (7.5 s instead of 10 s: a third more renewal writes), so of the three tries inside the 29 s the holder trusts any two may fail and the third confirm. New in the root entry and `@agboss/core/stores`: `StoreOutcomeUnknownError` (a store's call whose outcome is unknown: the base of what a store throws after a command timeout or a transport error), `StoreCallTimeoutError` and `StoreOutcomeSource`; a store package throws them with `source: Command`. A new counter, `agboss.instance.store_calls.timed_out`, and the log codes `store.call-timeout`, `execution.lease-abandoned` and `outbound-call.answer-unrecorded`. A store you list yourself is bounded too (a value given as a test `overrides` entry is not); `recoverApp()` bounds the reused stores again with the new instance's clock. No migration: a store that already answers within 15 s (a lease renewal within 5 s) never sees it. The store packages' own command timeouts follow in their releases.
